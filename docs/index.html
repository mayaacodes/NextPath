About NextPath

NextPath is designed for people rebuilding their lives after a major disruption—especially young adults facing barriers such as homelessness, financial hardship, interrupted education, unemployment, or limited access to opportunity. Rather than labeling people by what happened to them, NextPath focuses on where they are now and where they want to go.

How We Help

We connect you with supportive communities, local resources, and mentorship opportunities to help you chart your own path forward.
